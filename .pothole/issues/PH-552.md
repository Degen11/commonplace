# Global AI daily cap can be exhausted by a single IP, turning the spend guard into a site-wide outage

Confirmed by detect-security: Evidence resolves: one global 'global:ai-daily' bucket (_shared.js:187-189, default 2000 at :180) shared by identify (identify.js:135) and auto-group (auto-group.js:35) with only per-minute per-IP limits (identify 30, auto-group 15 at _shared.js:166-170); 45/min from one IP exhausts 2000 in about 45 minutes and then every user gets AI_DAILY_LIMIT. The _shared.js:176-179 comment and CLAUDE.md document the cap as a spend bound but say nothing about single-client exhaustion, so documented does not refute the cost.
